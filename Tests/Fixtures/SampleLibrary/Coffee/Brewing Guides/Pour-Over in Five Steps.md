# Pour-Over in Five Steps

[TOC]

## What you need

- [x] Dripper (V60 or similar)
- [x] Paper filter
- [x] Gooseneck kettle
- [ ] A scale (optional, but you'll want one)
- [ ] Patience

## The recipe

1. Heat water to **94 °C**.
2. Rinse the filter and discard the water.
3. Add 15 g of coffee, ground medium-fine.
4. Bloom: pour 45 g of water and wait 30 seconds.
5. Pour slowly in circles until you reach 250 g total.
   - Aim to finish by 2:30
   - Total drawdown around 3:00

## Ratio cheat sheet

| Cups | Coffee | Water |
|--:|--:|--:|
| 1 | 15 g | 250 g |
| 2 | 30 g | 500 g |
| 3 | 45 g | 750 g |
