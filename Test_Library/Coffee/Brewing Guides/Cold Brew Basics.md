# Cold Brew Basics

Cold brew is the most forgiving method there is. Coarse grind, cold water, time.

- Ratio: `1:8` coffee to water for a concentrate
- Steep: 14–18 hours in the fridge
- Filter twice: once through a mesh, once through paper

~~Hot water speeds it up~~ — don't. That's just iced coffee.

---

**Tip:** dilute the concentrate 1:1 with water or milk before serving.
